📊 Project completed: Cardiovascular Risk Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

I’m excited to share my CardioRisk dashboard, a Power BI project built to explore heart-disease patterns across clinical risk factors, medical history, demographics, and lifestyle categories.

🔎 What I built:
• A five-page interactive report: Executive Overview, Clinical Risk, History & Symptoms, Demographics, and Lifestyle
• KPI cards for total records, heart-disease cases/rate, average cholesterol, resting blood pressure, and age
• DAX measures and calculated categories
• Interactive Age Group, Region, and Sex slicers, plus a Reset Filters button
• Comparisons of heart-disease rates across risk-factor categories

📈 A few findings in this dataset:
• Overall heart-disease rate: 46.08%
• Hypertension group: 63.98% vs 30.22% for the no-hypertension group
• Stage 2 High blood-pressure category: 59.23% vs 24.43% for the Normal category

Tools: Power BI Desktop | Power Query | DAX | Data Visualization

This is a portfolio analytics project using a research-calibrated dataset, not real patient records or a clinical diagnostic tool. The percentages describe this dataset and should not be interpreted as causal or medical conclusions.
